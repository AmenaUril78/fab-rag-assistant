# Lockout/Tagout and Gas Leak Emergency Response

Document IDs: SAFE-LOTO-002, SAFE-EMR-010 | Area: All Areas | Owner: Environmental Health and Safety (EHS)
Note: Fictional sample document created for a portfolio project ("Example Fab"). Not real fab procedure.

## Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure
LOTO is required before any maintenance that exposes a person to electrical, RF, mechanical, pneumatic, or chemical energy.

1. Notify affected operators that the tool will be shut down.
2. Shut down the tool using the normal shutdown sequence.
3. Isolate all energy sources: main electrical breaker, RF generators, process gases, pneumatic supply, and chemical supply valves.
4. Apply your personal lock and tag to each isolation point. Every worker on the job applies their own lock.
5. Release stored energy: wait for capacitors to discharge (minimum 5 minutes for RF generators), bleed pneumatic lines, and purge gas lines with nitrogen.
6. Verify zero energy by attempting to start the tool and measuring voltage with a rated meter.
7. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.

## Toxic or Flammable Gas Leak Response
Fabs use hazardous gases such as silane (pyrophoric), arsine, phosphine, chlorine, and ammonia.

If a gas detector alarms (FAC-9001) or you smell an unusual odor:
1. Evacuate the area immediately using the nearest exit. Do not try to find or stop the leak.
2. Activate the nearest emergency alarm if it has not activated automatically.
3. Go to the designated assembly point and report to the area emergency coordinator.
4. Do not re-enter until the emergency response team declares the area safe.
Only trained emergency response team members wearing self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) may enter a gas leak area.

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Cleanroom garments are required in all fab areas. Additional PPE is required for chemical handling (face shield, apron, chemical gloves) and for maintenance on hot components (thermal gloves). Safety glasses are required in all equipment chase areas.
