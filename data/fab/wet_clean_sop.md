# Wet Bench Cleaning SOP

Document ID: WET-SOP-005 | Area: Wet Process | Owner: Wet Process Engineering
Note: Fictional sample document created for a portfolio project ("Example Fab"). Not real fab procedure.

## Scope
This SOP covers the standard RCA-style clean sequence and chemical bath management on automated wet benches.

## Clean Sequence
1. SC1 (ammonium hydroxide, hydrogen peroxide, water at 1:1:5) at 65 °C for 10 minutes. Removes particles and organic contamination.
2. Quick dump rinse (QDR) in deionized (DI) water.
3. Dilute HF (100:1) for 60 seconds. Removes native oxide.
4. QDR rinse.
5. SC2 (hydrochloric acid, hydrogen peroxide, water at 1:1:6) at 65 °C for 10 minutes. Removes metallic contamination.
6. Final rinse until DI water resistivity is above 15 MΩ·cm.
7. Spin rinse dry (SRD).

## Bath Life and Chemical Changes
- SC1 and SC2 baths: change every 8 hours or after 50 batches, whichever comes first. Peroxide decomposes at temperature, so bath concentration drifts over time.
- Dilute HF: change every 24 hours or when the etch rate monitor is outside ±5% of target.
Always record the chemical change time and operator badge number in the bath log.

## Particle Failures After Clean
If particle counts after wet clean exceed limits:
1. Check SC1 bath age and temperature.
2. Check the DI water resistivity and the final rinse time.
3. Inspect the SRD for worn wafer carriers.
4. Check filter differential pressure on the recirculation loop.

## Safety
Handle HF only with full chemical PPE: face shield, chemical apron, and HF-rated gloves. HF exposure may not cause immediate pain. Any suspected HF skin contact requires immediate rinsing and application of calcium gluconate gel, followed by a report to the emergency response team. Never mix waste streams from acid and base baths.
